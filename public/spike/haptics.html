Spike 1 — iOS haptics probe

Add to Home Screen, then open from the Home Screen icon and run each test. Report what you feel, not what you see.

Test A — direct tap on a real switch (control)

Tap the switch itself. This is the only path Apple officially supports. If this does nothing, haptics are unavailable on your device entirely.

Test B — programmatic, inside your tap

A button whose handler calls label.click() on a hidden switch. This is the ios-haptics technique. Documented to work on iOS 17.4–26.4 and to have been patched in 26.5.

Test C — programmatic, on an arbitrary later event

Same technique, but fired 1.5 s after your tap ends — simulating the bot's reply, a check, or a checkmate. This is the case the chess app actually needs.

Test D — navigator.vibrate

Reports whether the standard API even exists here. The app will never ship a silent call to it.
